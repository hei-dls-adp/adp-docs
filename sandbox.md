# Examples of exercises.

With Node-RED, you will:
From your home Wi-Fi:
    1.  Call a Python script to calculate a polynomial.
    2.  Generate a CSV file that you can open in Excel.
   
In general, try testing access to the PC from a smartphone on a **local wifi network** and draw the necessary conclusions in terms of OT security.
