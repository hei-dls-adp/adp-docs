## GMP and IQ/OQ/PQ Relationship

**Good Manufacturing Practice (GMP)** is a regulatory framework that ensures products are consistently produced and controlled to quality standards.

**IQ/OQ/PQ** (Installation Qualification, Operational Qualification, Performance Qualification) are validation methodologies that demonstrate GMP compliance:

- **IQ**: Verifies equipment is correctly installed per specifications (GMP requirement for equipment documentation)
- **OQ**: Confirms equipment operates within defined parameters under normal conditions (GMP operational controls)
- **PQ**: Demonstrates equipment consistently produces acceptable results under actual operating conditions (GMP process validation)

Together, they provide documented evidence that systems meet GMP requirements for validation, traceability, and quality assurance.
