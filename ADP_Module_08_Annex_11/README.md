## Annex 11: Reliability of Computerized Systems

EU GMP Annex 11 is an annex to the EU Good Manufacturing Practices.

It mandates that all computer systems used in pharmaceutical production be validated and secure.

It covers, in particular:

-   software validation
-   access management
-   audit trail
-   data integrity
-   backups
-   change management

## PAT en biotechnologies
PAT: Real-Time Process Control

Process Analytical Technology is an initiative launched by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration to encourage:

-   online or real-time measurement of critical parameters (CPPs)
-   monitoring of Critical Quality Attributes (CQAs)
-   scientific understanding of the process

Examples in biotechnology:
-   NIR or Raman spectroscopy in a bioreactor
-   online sensors for:
    -   glucose
    -   lactate
    -   biomass
    -   pH/O₂
-   multivariate models (chemometrics)

Objective:
➡ moving from final quality control to continuous process control.

## Consequence 
The link between PAT and Annex 11
PAT systems are almost always computerized: ➡ PAT systems must comply with Annex 11.
